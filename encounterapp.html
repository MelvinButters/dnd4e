How templates work: Select a template, then choose a monster from your loaded library or saved custom monsters. The template adjusts the monster's group role, defences, saves, HP, resistances, and adds template powers — then saves the result as a new custom monster in your library.
The original monster is never modified. Templates with variable resistances will prompt you to choose the damage type(s).
